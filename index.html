How It Works

A Simple Digital Lending Process

01 — Register

Create your secure account and provide your basic information.

02 — Verify

Complete identity and financial verification. Where permitted, we obtain your authorization before accessing relevant credit information through authorized providers.

03 — Apply

Select your desired loan amount and repayment period.

04 — Assessment

Our lending system evaluates your application, affordability and available credit information.

05 — Approval & Agreement

If approved, you’ll receive your loan offer and applicable terms for review before accepting.

06 — Receive Funds

After the required agreements and conditions are completed, funds are transferred using the supported payment process.

07 — Repay & Build Your Record

Make scheduled repayments through your account. Successful repayment history may be considered when determining eligibility for future financing.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much can I borrow?

Eligible applicants may apply for between $20,000 and $100,000, subject to credit assessment and applicable lending regulations.

What is the interest rate?

The platform’s proposed standard rate is 10% per annum, subject to applicable laws, disclosures and the final loan agreement.

Do I need collateral?

The proposed product is unsecured, meaning physical collateral is not required. Approval is subject to eligibility and credit assessment.

How long do I have to repay?

Available terms are proposed at 12 to 24 months, depending on the approved loan.

How is my credit history checked?

With the required authorization, relevant information may be verified through authorized credit-information providers and approved local partners.

What information do I need to apply?

Applicants may be required to provide identity, contact, residence, employment/income, banking/payment and other information necessary for KYC, affordability and credit assessment.

Which countries do you operate in?

The website should list only jurisdictions where the company or its relevant local entity/partner is legally authorized to provide the advertised service.

How do I make repayments?

Repayment methods available to a borrower will be displayed in their account. If your business is genuinely accepting only USDT and USDC, state that clearly in the applicable terms and disclosures.
